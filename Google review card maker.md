## Get your review link

1. Search for your business name on Google while signed in to the account that manages it.
2. Choose **Ask for reviews** on your Business Profile.
3. Copy the short link and paste it above.

You can also use a Place ID. [Find yours with Google's Place ID finder](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-id). Your Business Profile is at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com/).

## Before you print

- **Scan it first.** Point your phone camera at the preview or a test print to confirm it opens the review window.
- **Keep contrast high.** Dark QR on a white box scans best, so avoid pale custom QR colours.
- **Use High quality for print shops.** It doubles the resolution of the PNG and PDF.
- **Tables and waiting areas:** the table tent prints on one sheet and folds in the middle.
